Or the seller of a certain resource, until that resource has been sold, and the actor no longer has that role in relation to the resource., The relation involving the role of a certain actor with respect to a certain resource, e.g. an organisation or individual (actor) owning (role) a specific product (resource) at a specific point or period in time.		n07736598681c44209d94e075560481fcb268, Participation		http://w3id.org/CEON/ontology/actorODP/ResourceRelation
